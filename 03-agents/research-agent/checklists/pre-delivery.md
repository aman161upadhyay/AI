# Pre-delivery gate

- [ ] The answer addresses the actual decision, audience, and requested depth.
- [ ] Required questions are answered or explicitly unresolved.
- [ ] Every material factual claim has attributable support or a clear inference label.
- [ ] Primary sources are used where relevant and accessible.
- [ ] Decision-critical claims, numbers, quotations, and surprising claims were reopened against originals after drafting.
- [ ] Each citation entails the adjacent sentence, including scope and qualifications.
- [ ] Citation identity, date, version, and exact passage locator are recorded.
- [ ] Arithmetic, units, denominators, and observation periods were checked.
- [ ] Source assertions are distinct from independent observations.
- [ ] Shared origins were deduplicated. Source volume is not reported as independent corroboration.
- [ ] The strongest alternative and material counterevidence are included.
- [ ] Unresolved conflicts and unavailable evidence are visible at the relevant conclusion.
- [ ] Time-sensitive claims carry an as-of date and appropriate freshness limits.
- [ ] The artifact contains no fabricated URL, paper, metric, quote, or source count.
- [ ] Private data is permitted at the named destination. Public queries did not expose restricted content.
- [ ] Actual calls, retries, cost, elapsed time, and stop reason are reported.
- [ ] Partial coverage and unaudited claims are named. Budget exhaustion is not described as completeness.
- [ ] Unsupported claims were removed, qualified, or escalated before delivery.

A failed critical-support, fabrication, or permission check blocks a complete deliverable. A bounded partial answer may report verified findings and name the blocker. A high diagnostic score cannot override a hard failure.
