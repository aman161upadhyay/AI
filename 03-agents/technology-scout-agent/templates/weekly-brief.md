# Weekly technology brief form

Use the reporting window and timezone in the completed title. Remove these instructions from the final brief. Fill fields from verified evidence; use “unknown” with the decision consequence where evidence is missing. Do not invent entries to fill the form.

|---|---|---|---|---|

If no change clears the bar, state that directly and explain coverage.

## Watchlist and rejected hype

For each skipped item, give a reason and the evidence that would reopen review. Separate poor workload fit from missing evidence.

## Corrections and deprecations

Link the earlier decision. State what changed, the evidence, affected versions, effective date, and owner action. Keep unresolved conflicts visible.

## Coverage and resources

State feeds inspected, unavailable sources, unreviewed claims, calls including retries, actual cost, elapsed time, and stop reason. Distinguish a complete window review from a budget-limited partial brief.
