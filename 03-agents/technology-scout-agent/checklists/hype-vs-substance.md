# Hype versus substance

- [ ] The change addresses a named current problem.
- [ ] The original source was opened, not merely found in a snippet.
- [ ] Publication, release, tag, and observation dates are not conflated.
- [ ] Related announcements and wrappers are deduplicated by underlying change.
- [ ] Exact model/tool version and baseline are recorded.
- [ ] Availability and reproduction status are separate.
- [ ] Access restrictions, region/account scope, and hardware requirements are explicit.
- [ ] Code, weights, and data licenses are checked separately when relevant.
- [ ] Price basis includes units, settings, and observation date. Missing cost is unknown.
- [ ] Retention and privacy terms match the proposed data use.
- [ ] Benchmark tasks fit the workload. Baselines and inference settings are comparable.
- [ ] Sample size, judge, contamination, and reproducibility limits are recorded.
- [ ] Vendor assertions are distinct from independent and local results.
- [ ] Upvotes, stars, and rank are treated as discovery signals.
- [ ] Claimed gains are measured or explicitly labeled hypotheses.
- [ ] Strong counterevidence and failure cases are retained.
- [ ] Adopt has local acceptance evidence, compatibility checks, and a rollback plan.
- [ ] Trial has a hypothesis, fixed tasks, threshold, caps, and scoped authorization.
- [ ] Watch and Ignore include a concrete reopening condition.
- [ ] No package execution, private upload, spending, publication, or production change occurred without approval.

Return a decision with evidence IDs and unknowns. Do not use a high total score to waive a privacy or permission failure.
