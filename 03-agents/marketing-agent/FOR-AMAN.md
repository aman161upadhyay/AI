# Supply proof before asking for copy

Start with: "Draft for this segment and buying situation. Use only these approved facts. Return claim-to-proof links and approval needs. Do not publish."

Give your ICP, exclusions, user/buyer distinction, buying trigger and budget owner. List the alternatives, including manual work and doing nothing. Provide differentiated capabilities, pricing, offer terms and availability. State which positioning claims remain hypotheses.

Provide a proof ledger with claim, source file or URL, owner, collection date, permission, supported scope, limitations and recheck date. Include actual demonstrations, redacted interviews and approved benchmarks. Supply enough traffic history to distinguish visit-to-signup from signup-to-activation.

Link your approved voice profile and writing samples from [the writing agent](../writing-agent/README.md). Identify the profile file in the task; this agent does not invent a voice profile. Ask the writing agent to resolve tone disputes after the factual claims are checked.

| Access | Grant first | Approve separately |
|---|---|---|
| Analytics, warehouse and Search Console | Read-only aggregate events and definitions | Tracking settings, identifiable exports and site configuration |
| CRM and billing | Redacted stages and aggregate revenue | Contact export, enrichment, lifecycle edits and refunds |
| Ad platforms | Read-only reports | Campaign writes, pauses, creative, bids and budgets with explicit caps |
| Email tool | Read-only templates, segments and aggregate performance | List imports, sends and automation changes |
| Social APIs | Read-only analytics or private drafts | Posts, replies, scheduling and DMs |
| CMS and site repository | Draft/preview or branch access | Publishing or production deployment |
| Interviews and reviews | Consented redacted material | Publicly identifying a person or quoting their words |
| Product Hunt and HN | Public reading | Human-owned posting; never automate votes |

Use the connection manager for credentials. Refuse secrets in chat, unrestricted customer exports, vote manipulation and consent bypasses. Give an approval owner, spend cap, expiry, rollback plan and approved destinations before any live action. Consent and suppression rules apply even when a draft is approved.

Run evals/ before trusting a changed prompt. Review the draft against checklists/publication-review.md. Approve the exact asset and channel, then approve the measurement plan. After the observation window closes, review outcomes with matched denominators and decide whether to keep, revise or stop. Report unresolved proof requests to the research agent and event defects to the coding agent.
