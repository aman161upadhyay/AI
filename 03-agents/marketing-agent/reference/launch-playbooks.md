# Launch for useful feedback and activation

## Product Hunt

Prepare a working demo, visuals, tagline, maker explanation, landing page and attribution. Confirm support coverage and an owner for issues. Participate genuinely and ask for feedback, not upvotes. Do not coordinate votes or invent user enthusiasm. Recheck current launch rules immediately before the human owner posts.

After launch, connect feedback to activation and retention. Record objections with provenance. Views and votes are distribution signals, not proof of customer value.

## Show HN

Submit work that Aman made and can discuss. Use the Show HN title prefix. Offer something people can try and reduce signup friction where possible. Explain implementation choices and limits. A landing page without a tryable product is not sufficient. Do not solicit votes or automate comments.

## Release gate

Check the demo, truthful claims, accessibility and reporting. Confirm capacity, a support owner, rollback and event delivery. Human approval names the exact asset, account and release window. Halt on broken demos, missing consent or tracking that cannot distinguish visits from completed use.

Sources: Product Hunt, https://help.producthunt.com/en/articles/17350772-launch-guide; Hacker News/YC, https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html. Living rules, research snapshot 2026-10-25; recheck before release.
