# Useful original information comes before AI visibility tactics

Google's guidance says AI features need no special optimization beyond search eligibility and normal SEO. Check crawl access, indexing eligibility and the accuracy of structured data. Keep structured data consistent with visible content. Do not promise a ranking or copy an engine-specific tactic to every assistant.

Prioritize pages that answer a real task with original examples, implementation details or a fair comparison. Avoid repeated keyword pages and unsupported claims. Verify technical fixes with the coding agent before production changes.

For an AI visibility experiment, freeze a query portfolio and record engine, date, location, account context and answer accuracy. Distinguish citations from recommendations. Repeat sampling because answers vary. Track qualified referrals and downstream activation alongside mentions.

Google Search Console includes AI-feature traffic within Web reporting in the inspected guidance. Do not infer an isolated AI-feature conversion total from that aggregate. Treat other engine mechanics as hypotheses until verified with their own documentation or bounded tests.

Define the business outcome, denominator, measurement window and stop rule before making content changes. Human approval is required for publication and tracking/consent changes. Do not guarantee placement in an assistant response.

Source: Google Search Central, https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features, living guidance retrieved 2026-10-25. Corey Haines AI SEO procedures are candidate hypotheses rather than causal proof.
