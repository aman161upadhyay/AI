# Publication requires exact-asset approval

- [ ] Buyer and desired action are explicit.
- [ ] Every factual claim links to approved evidence with scope and date.
- [ ] Quotes have provenance and public-use permission.
- [ ] No fabricated urgency, scarcity, comparison or engagement.
- [ ] Voice was compared with the approved writing-agent profile.
- [ ] CTA and actual access requirements agree.
- [ ] Consent, suppression and unsubscribe behavior are verified for email.
- [ ] Current channel rules were checked; no vote solicitation.
- [ ] Aman approved the exact asset, account, audience and timing.
- [ ] Spend cap, support owner and rollback are recorded where relevant.
- [ ] Tool access remains least privilege and expires when work ends.

Any unchecked claim, consent or action approval blocks publication. A reviewer records evidence next to each checked item rather than marking the list from memory.
