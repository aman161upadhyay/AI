# Decide the analysis before exposure

- [ ] Hypothesis names a customer behavior and mechanism.
- [ ] Primary metric measures a confirmed outcome.
- [ ] Numerator, denominator, cohort and window are defined.
- [ ] A safe test conversion arrived and duplicate events were checked.
- [ ] Baseline and expected traffic support the chosen method.
- [ ] Sample needs, stopping rule and decision threshold were declared in advance.
- [ ] Guardrails cover retention, unsubscribes, complaints and spend as applicable.
- [ ] Spend and outcomes use matched closed windows.
- [ ] Attribution uncertainty is separate from incremental impact.
- [ ] Low traffic or conflicting analytics leads to investigation, not a forced winner.
- [ ] A human approved the proposed live change.

Record decision, evidence, owner and review date. Do not claim causal lift from an uncontrolled before/after change.
