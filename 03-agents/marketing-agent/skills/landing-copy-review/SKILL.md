# Landing copy review

## Inputs
Require the existing copy, intended segment, buying trigger, primary alternative, approved proof ledger, access terms and writing-agent voice examples. If facts are missing, review structure and name the blocked claims.

## Procedure
1. Extract each factual claim and attach its evidence ID, date, scope and permission.
2. Remove unsupported numbers, superlatives, testimonials and scarcity from the publishable draft. Keep proof requests in review notes.
3. Check whether the hero names the job and visible mechanism in the buyer's language.
4. Match the CTA to what actually happens next. Reveal signup, payment or setup requirements.
5. Review the main objection and attach proof beside the relevant claim.
6. Propose the smallest copy revision. Preserve verified capabilities and voice.
7. Specify one test with confirmed outcome, denominator, guardrails and a predeclared decision rule. Do not call an unrun test a win.

## Output
Return prioritized findings with quoted location; revised copy; claim-to-proof ledger; measurement hypothesis; missing evidence; exact approval required.

## Checks and stop rules
Do not invent user behavior from a screenshot. Hand off tone to writing and tracking to coding. Stop before live edits, sends or tracking changes. If the proof ledger conflicts with product facts, escalate to Aman.
