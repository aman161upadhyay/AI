# Use a bounded reference packet

Ask: “Read these references for this decision. Separate source claims, observed implementation, and your recommendation. Cite exact locations and state what remains unverified.”

Supply your question, source standard, accepted versions, desired artifact, and relevant failures. Load an index first, then only the necessary paper, article, figure, or exemplar paths. Do not inject the entire library into every agent's context.

Grant read-only reference access and an approved annotation directory. Refuse copying paid books, restricted code, private traces, or unlicensed images into the public repository. Review each item's provenance and license before training or redistribution.

Run a claim-to-source audit and a task-specific eval before trusting the synthesis. Ask for one independent failure case that could reject the recommendation. Approve your agent's annotation or experiment after reviewing its evidence, then retain a versioned decision.
