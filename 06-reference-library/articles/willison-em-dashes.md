## Summary

Willison describes accusations that his blog prose came from an LLM. His publishing code automatically converted spaced hyphens into em dashes by at least 2015. The behavior predates ChatGPT and explains the punctuation without AI authorship.

The post is a concrete counterexample to a punctuation-based accusation. It does not establish the precision or recall of any general detector.

## What the writing agent takes

Use punctuation as a style choice, not evidence of origin. Respect demonstrated habits when the governing writing rules permit them. Repository prose follows its no-em-dash convention, but that convention is not a scientific test.
