## Summary

The original text favors concrete nouns, active verbs, removal of unnecessary words, and placement of related words close together. Sentence endings can carry emphasis. Concision should remove work that serves no purpose rather than erase distinctions the reader needs.

These are craft rules with contextual exceptions. The fetched edition does not authorize attributing advice added in White's later revision to Strunk's original.

## What the writing agent takes

Inspect ambiguous references and long interruptions between related words. End a sentence on the information that deserves attention. Preserve a useful preposition or "that" when removal makes the grammar harder to follow. Never infer an actor merely to create an active sentence.
