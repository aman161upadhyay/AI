# Using the Wikipedia adaptation

Load the adaptation with its source notice when reviewing an encyclopedia-style draft. Supply the intended genre and the draft's source material. Grant read-only access to cited sources; do not grant publication or file-deletion authority.

Ask the agent to identify unsupported claims and verify citations before editing stylistic cues. Require the original sentence, proposed edit, and reason. Do not infer authorship from punctuation or vocabulary.

Check a sample of edits against the sources and retain valid genre-specific formatting. Before redistribution, retain contributor attribution and the revision URL, describe changes, and apply the CC BY-SA 4.0 terms to adaptations. Do not use this guide as experimental evidence or a detector.
