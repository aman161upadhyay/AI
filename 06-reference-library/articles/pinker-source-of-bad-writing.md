# The Source of Bad Writing

Author: Steven Pinker.
URL: https://stevenpinker.com/files/pinker/files/the_source_of_bad_writing_-_wsj_0.pdf
Date: 2014-09-25, Wall Street Journal essay, author-hosted copy. Accessed 2026-10-25.
Licence note: Original summary of a copyrighted essay. The complete book The Sense of Style was not inspected or copied.

## Summary

The curse of knowledge makes writers assume that readers share their context. Experts can omit definitions or inferential steps because those steps feel obvious to them. Dense language often reflects a mismatch between the writer's knowledge and the reader's rather than a need for more impressive vocabulary.

The practical remedy is to model the intended reader and obtain feedback from someone with similar knowledge. A familiar colleague who knows the project may fail to reveal missing context.

## What the writing agent takes

Ask what the audience already knows. Define a necessary unfamiliar term once. Make causal and logical transitions explicit. Give a fresh reader only the draft and ask them to explain the argument. Do not add introductory material that this actual reader already understands.
