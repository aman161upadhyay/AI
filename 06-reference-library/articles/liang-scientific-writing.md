# Mapping the Increasing Use of LLMs in Scientific Papers

## Summary

The paper estimates LLM-modified sentence proportions across scientific abstracts and introductions, including arXiv categories, bioRxiv, and a Nature portfolio. It uses synthetic GPT-3.5 rewrites to estimate distributions and tests calibration on mixtures.

Computer-science abstract estimates rise from 2.3% in November 2022 to 17.5% in February 2024. Reported validation prediction errors are below 3.5 percentage points at population level in the tested mixtures.

The nonzero baseline and assumptions about distribution shifts belong beside the result. Correlations with posting frequency, crowded topics, and shorter papers do not establish causes. The unit is modified sentences, not fraudulent papers or wholly generated abstracts.

## What the writing agent takes

Preserve the measurement unit and calibration assumptions. Do not turn a population estimate into an individual detector. Use this study to understand aggregate changes in prose, not to justify accusations about a draft.
