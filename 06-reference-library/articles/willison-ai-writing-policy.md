# My current policy on AI writing for my blog

Author: Simon Willison.

## Summary

Willison reserves text expressing his opinions or speaking as "I" for his own writing. He uses LLMs for proofreading and allows generated code documentation, but edits away invented opinions and rationale. A claim that a design exists to improve maintainability can be a fabricated explanation even when the code description is accurate.

This is a practitioner's authorship policy, not a universal disclosure rule. Aman must choose his own policy within the publication's requirements.

## What the writing agent takes

Require explicit authorization for first-person claims and opinions. Do not manufacture design intent from an implementation. Keep drafting permission separate from publication and disclosure decisions.
