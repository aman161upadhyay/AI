# Writes and Write-Nots

Author: Paul Graham.
URL: https://www.paulgraham.com/writes.html
Date: October 2024. Accessed 2026-10-25.

## Summary

Graham argues that writing requires clear thinking and that outsourcing the work can remove the pressure to learn it. He predicts a widening separation between people who choose to practice writing and those who depend on tools.

"To write well you have to think clearly, and thinking clearly is hard."

The essay's social forecast is an argument, not a measured outcome. Its value for this agent is the distinction between help with expression and replacing the author's intellectual work.

## What the writing agent takes

Interview Aman for his thesis and reasoning before drafting. Let objections revise the thesis. Never infer his convictions from a style corpus. The agent should expose gaps in an argument rather than hide them under fluent prose.
