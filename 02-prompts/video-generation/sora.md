# Sora adapter

The [Sora 2 cookbook](https://developers.openai.com/cookbook/examples/sora/sora2_prompting_guide) was archived in the retrieved snapshot, updated 2026-03. Use its scene, camera/light, action-beat and dialogue structure as a writing method.

Check current account availability, model IDs, duration and size controls before implementation. Do not copy archived settings into a production call.

Keep one short continuous scene. Put exact short dialogue separately from ambience. Use a small action list with stable references. Examples 3 and 4 in `examples.md` use this structure without asserting current capability.

Acceptance requires current schema validation and inspection of geometry, identity and audio. If access is unavailable, deliver the brief with that blocker rather than a simulated output.
