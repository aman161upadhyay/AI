# Five complete shot briefs

These fictional practice prompts come from research 05. They were authored, not generated or graded. Assets described as approved must be supplied and rights-cleared before execution. Settings are requests subject to current provider controls.

## 1. Veo product detail

```text
Create one 8-second 16:9 product-detail shot using the approved first-frame
image of our brushed-steel kettle. Medium close-up at countertop height,
50mm lens feel. The camera dollies slowly 20 centimeters left to right.
The kettle stays still while a thin stream of steam rises from its spout.
Walnut counter, ivory plaster wall, no other objects moving. Soft overcast
window light from camera left; silver, ivory and walnut palette. Photoreal
materials with restrained contrast. Ambient noise: quiet kitchen room tone.
SFX: gentle simmer and faint metal ticking. No dialogue or music. Preserve
the reference's handle, spout and reflections; leave the right third calm
for later editorial text. Add no lettering to the generated footage.
```

## 2. Veo outdoor-app establishing shot

```text
Generate one 8-second vertical 9:16 establishing shot for an outdoor route
planner. Begin in a wide eye-level view of one adult hiker standing safely
on a broad marked path in a misty pine valley. The hiker adjusts a backpack
strap once, then looks toward the trail. Slowly push the camera forward;
keep the horizon level. Diffuse dawn light, slate blue mist, pine green and
muted orange pack. Natural documentary realism. Ambient noise: wind through
pines and distant birds. SFX: one strap buckle click. No speech or score.
Keep the hiker's clothing and position continuous. Frame the upper third
with quiet mist for a title added in post. No perilous cliff or abrupt cut.
```

## 3. Sora-style craft studio

```text
One continuous short shot, intended length 8 seconds, horizontal composition.
A ceramic artist's hands work a small clay bowl at a stationary studio table.
Use the approved hands-and-bowl reference; no face is required.
Cinematography: close-up, 65mm lens feel, locked tripod, shallow but sufficient
depth of field to keep the rim and fingertips legible.
Lighting: broad side window, warm bounce, matte clay, oatmeal linen, dark wood.
Action beats: hands steady the bowl; the right thumb smooths one short section
of the rim; both hands relax beside it. Keep the bowl's geometry continuous.
Sound: soft clay friction and quiet room tone. Dialogue: none. Music: none.
Visual style: tactile observational film with subtle grain, no title graphics.
Use the provider's current duration and size controls rather than interpreting
those settings from this prose.
```

## 4. Sora-style transit information film

```text
One short medium shot, intended length 8 seconds, 16:9. An adult station
attendant in a plain navy jacket stands beside a clean information desk.
Eye-level camera, locked framing, 40mm lens feel. Soft overhead practical
lighting with neutral skin tones; navy, warm gray and cream palette.
Action: the attendant makes one small open-hand gesture toward the desk,
then returns to a relaxed pose. Background people remain softly out of focus.
Dialogue, attendant only: "The next train leaves from platform four."
Audio: clear natural voice, low station ambience, no announcement competing
with the line, no music. No captions burned into the image. Keep the jacket,
desk and face stable. This is a fictional staged scene, not a real transit alert.
```

## 5. Runway paper mascot

```text
Input: the approved still image of the folded-paper bird on the blue desk.
Generate a single 5-second scene. The bird tilts its head slightly to the
left, returns to its starting pose, and holds still. One paper wing makes a
small settling movement. The camera remains locked. The desk and background
stay still. Movement is delicate and physical, like carefully animated paper.
```

For each shot, inspect identity, geometry, action, camera, lighting, audio if supported, rights and cut/loop. Record actual settings and cost. Do not add more “verified” examples without generated output and a review record.
