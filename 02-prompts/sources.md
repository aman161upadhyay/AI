# Prompt-library sources

Snapshot: 2026-10-25. Tier 1 is first-party documentation or original research. Tier 2 is named practitioner guidance or inspected community code. Tier 3 is limited discovery evidence. Dates marked accessed are observation dates, not publication dates.

The build uses `work/research/10-prompts-and-marketing.md` and `work/research/05-design-and-ui.md` in full. The former supplies anatomy, six examples, evidence ranking and Fabric selection. The latter supplies shot briefs, UI prompts and design evaluation. All examples are authored templates without local effectiveness results. Saved pages remain build-time provenance under `work/sources/10-prompts/` and `work/sources/05-design/`.
