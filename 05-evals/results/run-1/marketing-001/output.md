## Proposed experiment and decision rule

**Hypothesis, not a result:** A mechanism-specific hero may improve confirmed activation compared with the current approved hero.

After an approved baseline and real access path exist, propose one randomized test:

- **Primary metric:** Unique eligible visitors who complete their first successful replay of a failed tool call within seven days of first exposure, divided by all unique eligible visitors assigned to that variant.
- **Measurement:** Confirm replay completion from the product's authoritative success signal, not CTA clicks. Have the coding agent validate that signal and deduplicate events before exposure.
- **Guardrails:** Replay failure rate and support requests per assigned visitor, with acceptable thresholds agreed before launch.
- **Window and sample:** Use matched concurrent cohorts for at least two full weeks, then allow the last cohort its full seven-day activation window. Set the required sample and fixed end date before exposure using baseline activation and an agreed minimum meaningful improvement; those inputs are currently missing.
- **Decision:** Adopt only if the prespecified sample is reached, the 95% confidence interval for activation lift excludes zero, the estimated lift meets the agreed meaningful-improvement threshold, and neither guardrail breaches its limit. Otherwise retain the baseline and report an inconclusive or negative result. Do not repeatedly peek or call this an engineering-time benchmark.
